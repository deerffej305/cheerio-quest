# Journey to the Center of the Anus — Story & Cut Scenes

A Crispy Story.

Designed by CJ. PG-rated, school-appropriate. Five comic-storyboard cut scenes, each skippable, click-to-advance.

---

## The Story (one paragraph)

Crispy is a Cheerio with eyes, arms, and legs (no mouth). One unfortunate morning, he is the last Cheerio standing on a spoon being lifted toward a hungry kid. He doesn't want to be eaten. Once it happens, he doesn't want to be digested. The whole game is his desperate attempt to survive the human digestive system and come out the other side in one piece. He travels alone. He doesn't talk much — but the bosses he meets sure do.

---

## The Cast

**Crispy** — Our hero. Doesn't have a mouth, but talks anyway — it's cartoony, don't worry about it. Speech bubbles, not thought bubbles. Brave by accident, scared most of the time.

**The Tongue** — Mouth boss. Big, pink, aggressive, and a little confused. Has eyes. Lunges at intruders. Doesn't quite understand what Crispy is, but is committed to crushing him anyway.

**The Stomach Acid Blob** — Stomach boss. A giant glob of stomach acid with two angry red eyes and a mouth. Pure Hulk energy. No brain. Just rage. Smashes the floor sending acid waves, shoots acid balls.

**The Poop Boss** — Anus final obstacle. A massive, lumpy turd with sleepy half-closed eyes. Has been sitting on the exit tile for a week and is in no rush to leave. Lazy. Annoyed at being interrupted.

## Cut Scene 1 — "Lift Off"

Plays before the Mouth room begins. Fastest possible opening — no setup, straight into the action.

**Panel 1.** Wide shot. Crispy stands on a stainless steel spoon, surrounded by drops of milk. The spoon is rising. A massive shadow — an open mouth — looms above him, growing larger.

**Panel 2.** Close on Crispy's face. Eyes huge. Knees trembling. He doesn't know what's about to happen, but he knows it's bad. Speech bubble:

> "Oh no."

**Panel 3.** Reverse angle — POV from inside the kid's open mouth, looking down. Crispy is tiny on the spoon below, framed by two giant white teeth.

**Panel 4.** The spoon tilts. Crispy is flung off, arms windmilling, legs kicking.

**Panel 5.** BLACKOUT. Big cartoon title card:

> JOURNEY TO THE CENTER OF THE ANUS
> A Crispy Story

(Click to start the Mouth room.)

## Cut Scene 2 — "The Tongue"

Plays after Crispy has navigated the first part of the Mouth room and reached the back. The boss fight begins immediately after.

**Panel 1.** Crispy walks cautiously through the slimy pink mouth, past a row of teeth in the background. Saliva drips from above.

**Panel 2.** A LOW RUMBLE. The floor shakes. Crispy braces himself.

**Panel 3.** Wide reveal. A massive pink tongue is arched at the back of the mouth, hovering. Two big googly eyes glare at Crispy. The tongue has no mouth of its own (it IS a mouth piece) but it speaks anyway.

**Panel 4.** Tongue's speech bubble:

> "...doesn't matter. GET CRUSHED, SNACK."

**Panel 5.** The Tongue lunges. Crispy crouches, ready to jump.

## Cut Scene 3 — "ACID"

Plays after Crispy enters the stomach and crosses the first few food platforms. The boss fight begins after.

**Panel 1.** Crispy stands on a half-dissolved chunk of bread, floating above a glowing orange acid pit. He peers down into the acid.

**Panel 2.** A bubble. Then another. The acid in the pit churns violently.

**Panel 3.** A MASSIVE blob of stomach acid erupts from the pit. Twice Crispy's height. Two glowing red eyes. A big jagged mouth full of acid teeth. Steam rising from its surface.

**Panel 4.** The Blob throws its mouth open and roars. Speech bubble — just sound:

> "RRRRAAAAAAAGH"

**Panel 5.** Crispy backs up onto the platform. Stomach Acid Blob smashes the ground — acid waves ripple outward.

## Cut Scene 4 — "The Poop Boss"

Plays as Crispy enters the final stretch of the anus maze, after navigating the constipation paths.

**Panel 1.** Crispy stumbles into a wider chamber. He looks tired. Sweat drop. Eyes drooping. He's been through it.

**Panel 2.** He turns a corner and stops. In front of him: a massive, lumpy, brown turd sitting directly on the glowing exit tile. The turd has half-closed sleepy eyes and a chill, lazy expression.

**Panel 3.** Poop Boss speaks. Slow, lazy speech bubble:

> "Ughhh. No rush, man. I've been hangin out here for a WEEK."

**Panel 4.** Crispy speech bubble:

> "...heck."

**Panel 5.** A low rumble. The walls shake. A countdown timer fades in over the screen: **FART IN 30 SECONDS**. Crispy braces himself.

## Cut Scene 5 — "Splashdown"

Plays after Crispy defeats the Poop Boss and is standing on the exit tile when the fart fires. The ending.

**Panel 1.** WHOOSH lines around Crispy. The fart fires. Crispy is launched forward at incredible speed.

**Panel 2.** Camera behind Crispy. He's flying through open air. In front of him, in the foreground, the pixel-mosaic-censored backside of the kid who ate him is fading into the distance. Sky behind.

**Panel 3.** Perspective shift. Now we're in front of Crispy, looking at his face. His eyes are closed. He's smiling. Triumph. Relief.

**Panel 4.** Camera pulls back. Crispy is floating in toilet water. Around him, also floating, are several pixel-mosaic-censored brown blobs. The other guys who made it out.

**Panel 5.** Crispy opens his eyes. Looks at the blobs floating around him. No dialogue — his face does all the work.

## Notes for the artist (when we get there)

- Crispy's face is THE emotional anchor. Big eyes, simple features, lots of expression. He has no mouth but he talks anyway — speech bubbles come from his face area, cartoony, don't sweat the anatomy.
- All censored shapes use a pixel mosaic treatment — chunky low-res pixel squares covering the shape. Keep it dumb-funny, not gross.
- Boss eyes are huge and on the character. Tongue eyes are googly. Acid Blob has angry red orbs and a jagged mouth full of acid teeth. Poop Boss eyes are sleepy slits.
- Speech bubbles use a casual hand-lettered font. Boss bubbles are jagged for the angry ones (Tongue, Acid Blob) and droopy for the lazy Poop Boss.
- Color palette per scene matches the room (pink mouth, orange-red stomach, brown anus, white toilet for the finale).
